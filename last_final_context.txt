**4. DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE UPSTREAM MANUFACTURING PROCESS > 4.1. Overview of the Upstream Manufacturing Process**

The OCU400 DS is produced in HEK293 suspension cell culture using a production bioreactor-based manufacturing process. HEK293 cells are initially recovered and cultivated in an Erlenmeyer shake flask to initiate the seed train. The cell culture is expanded from the HEK293SF-3F6 working cell bank (WCB) and is performed through sequential scale-up of the culture volume. Following recovery, the cells are expanded through progressively larger shake flask cultures and intermediate seed expansion steps until the required cell biomass is achieved for inoculation of the production bioreactor. The upstream process is designed to support manufacturing across campaigns while maintaining consistent culture conditions and process performance. The upstream process flow for OCU400 production from GLP to PPQ scale is represented in Figure 3.

**4. DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE UPSTREAM MANUFACTURING PROCESS > 4.2. Production of Plasmids (Transgene, Helper, and Rep/Cap)**

The production of OCU400 (AAV5-hNR2E3) is based on triple plasmid transfection in HEK293 suspension cells with transgene phNR2E3, pRep2/Cap5, and pHelper plasmids. The phNR2E3 transgene plasmid is designed, synthesized, and characterized for identity and integrity of various regulatory elements (AAV2-ITR, CMV enhancer, CBA promoter, chimeric intron from rabbit globulin gene, and SV40 poly A). The rep gene sequence consists of wild type AAV2 serotype, and the cap gene sequence consists of wild type AAV5 serotype. The pHelper plasmid is used to provide various factors for AAV5 production in HEK293 cells. During research and development, Ampicillin antibiotic cassette was replaced with a kanamycin antibiotic cassette in all the plasmids (phNR2E3, pRep2/Cap5 and pHelper plasmids) and characterized for identity in plasmids produced during cGMP production. The purified plasmids were released against the specification.
[type=paragraph, section=4. DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE UPSTREAM MANUFACTURING PROCESS > 4.2. Production of Plasmids (Transgene, Helper, and Rep/Cap), pages=30]

A feeding system is welded to the bioreactor bag and the pooled cell seed from passage 4 is inoculated to the bag using the compressed air. After 10-30 minutes of starting the culture, a sample is removed using a 50 mL sterile syringe for measuring cell density and viability.

**5. DOWNSTREAM PRODUCTION OF OCU400 (AAV5- HNR2E3) > 5.1. Overview of Downstream Production Process**

The production bioreactor culture is collected into sterile centrifuge bottles and centrifuged for a specified time at 2-8℃ for harvesting. The cell pellet are resuspended with lysis buffer stored at ≤-60℃ for ≥ 72 hours. Frozen cell harvests are thawed in water bath at 37 ± 1 ℃ and treated with biolonase to digest host cell DNA and plasmid DNA. After conductivity adjustment with IEX buffer, the crude cell lysate is clarified by centrifugation and depth filtration and further processed using a 0.65/0.2-micron filter to reduce bioburden. The clarified lysate is purified using Capto AVB affinity chromatography in a bind and elute mode. OCU400 (AAV5-hNR2E3) is eluted at low pH, the eluate was concentrated by TFF (TFF-1) and further purified by density gradient ultra-centrifugation. OCU400 (AAV5- hNR2E3) is collected and concentrated to a designated viral particle concentration (Vg) by TFF (TFF-2) and formulated by diafiltration with the formulation buffer. An overview diagram for the downstream process is shown in Figure 4.
